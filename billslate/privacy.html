Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Billslate ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses and stores information when it is installed on a Jira Cloud site. The app is developed and operated by Gaku Labs (an individual developer). You can contact us at gaku.labs@gmail.com.

Where your data is stored

Billslate is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure. All data the app stores is kept in Atlassian's hosted storage for Forge apps. The app has no servers of its own and does not send your data to any service outside Atlassian.

Information we collect

When Billslate is installed on your Jira site, it stores:

  • Worklogs — a copy of the time logged in Jira: the worklog and work item IDs, the Atlassian account ID of the person who logged the time, when the work started, and how long it took. The worklog's comment is not stored.
  • Work items — for work items that have logged time: the key, summary, project, parent work item, hierarchy level (for example epic or subtask), and remaining estimate.
  • Hourly rates — the rate you set for each person (by Atlassian account ID) and the date it applies from.
  • Budgets and settings — the budgets you create, the currency, and the Jira groups allowed to see amounts.

People's names are read from Jira when a page is shown and are not stored by the app.

How we use this information

We use this information only to provide the app's features: turning logged time into amounts using your hourly rates, comparing them with your budgets, and exporting line items for invoicing. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

Who can see amounts

Hourly rates, costs and budgets are shown only to Jira administrators and to members of the Jira groups that an administrator chooses in the app's settings. This check is made by the app's backend, not only in the page shown to the user.

How long we keep it

The data is kept while the app is installed, since it forms part of your own business records. When the app is uninstalled, its stored data is deleted by Atlassian in line with Atlassian's Forge data retention rules. If a worklog or work item is deleted in Jira, the app removes its copy.

Your rights

Site administrators can contact us at gaku.labs@gmail.com to ask about, export or delete the data the app holds for their site. Individuals can ask their Jira site administrator, who controls the data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
